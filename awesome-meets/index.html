Meeting code

The few rules that keep this nice for everyone.

  • Show up. If something comes up, cancel at least 24 hours ahead.
  • Lunch is 45 minutes. After-work is one drink or an hour.
  • Everyone pays for themselves.
  • No pitching.
  • Use whatever language works for you both. Be curious, be kind.

How we handle your data

The Awesome Marketers (Anna Pogrebniak) collects these answers only to match you with other members of Awesome Meets.

  • Where it's stored: a private Google Sheet in The Awesome Marketers' Google Workspace. Only the organiser can open it.
  • Who sees what: your matches see your name, job title, company, LinkedIn link and the topics you picked. Topics marked 🔒 are only shown to a match who picked the same topic.
  • Legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • How long: until you leave Awesome Meets, and at most 12 months after your last meeting.
  • Your rights: ask to see, correct or delete your answers at anna@awesomemarketers.fi. You can also complain to the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman.
