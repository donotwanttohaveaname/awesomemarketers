Hey awesome marketers 🧡

Let's try something new. We've preached for years that networking is everything, and now after talking to so many of you, I wanna connect you in person.

The concept: you fill in this questionnaire and I match you with 1 or 2 marketers in Helsinki. You then meet that person (or two) over lunch or an after-work coffee in the next four weeks.

This is the first test. If we decide to keep the format, we'll send you a new invitation.

It's a very informal way to meet more people and grow your network.

Your data in Awesome Autumn Meets

  • Who has access: only me, Anna. No one else can see your answers. I use Claude, an AI assistant, to help me find good matches.
  • What it's used for: matching you with 1 or 2 marketers. Nothing else.
  • How you'll hear from me: when you're matched, I send you a message on LinkedIn and by email with the place, date and time. From there it's up to you and your match to continue.
  • After the meetup: I send you a short follow-up questionnaire asking for your feedback.
  • 🔒 topics: never shared with your match, unless you both picked the same one.
  • Where it's stored: a private Google Sheet in The Awesome Marketers' Google Workspace.
  • How long: deleted within 12 months, or sooner if you ask.
  • Your rights: this is based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. Ask me to see, correct or delete your answers at anna@awesomemarketers.fi. You can also complain to the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman.
