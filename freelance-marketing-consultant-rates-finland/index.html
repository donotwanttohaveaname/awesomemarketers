How much does a freelance marketing consultant charge in Finland?

A freelance marketing consultant in Finland charges €520 to €650 per day, starting from €435. Ad-hoc hourly work runs €70 to €115 per hour, starting from €60. The starting figure is the 2026 median gross salary of €4,658 per month from the Awesome Marketers Marketing Salary Survey Finland 2026 (148 respondents), converted to a freelance rate with holiday pay and YEL pension included: your break-even.

New freelancers on the discounted starter YEL rate break even slightly lower, from €405 per day or €55 per hour.

To be blunt: the starting figures are bare break-even. They tell you what it costs to be your own employer at the median salary, nothing more. The ranges are what established freelancers actually quote: break-even plus 20 to 50% on day rates, and plus 20 to 100% for ad-hoc hourly, because single hours carry the most overhead. If a client tells you €435 sounds expensive, they are comparing it to a salary. It is not a salary. It is a salary plus pension, holidays, and risk.

02🧮

How we calculated these rates

We calculated these freelance rates the same way our freelance rate calculator does, starting from real salary data instead of a guess. The method, step by step:

  1. Start with the employee's monthly gross salary. Overall median in 2026: €4,658.
  2. Add holiday pay. Multiply by 14/11, because employees get roughly 14 months of pay over 11 working months (holiday pay plus the holiday bonus).
  3. Add YEL pension. Divide by 0.76 at the standard ~24% YEL rate. New freelancers get a starter discount of ~19%, so divide by 0.81 instead.
  4. Divide by 18 billable days per month. That is 21 working days minus roughly one public holiday, one sick day, and one admin day.
  5. Divide by 7.5 hours for the break-even hourly rate, then round to the nearest €5.

All figures exclude VAT. The result is your bare break-even, the starting figure in every table: what you need to match an employed peer, nothing more. The day-rate range adds 20 to 50% on top of it; the ad-hoc hourly range adds 20 to 100%, because single hours are the most expensive way to hire anyone and booking full days is the discount. Marketing strategy tops the table at €85 to €145 per hour for ad-hoc work, from a €70 baseline, and it is not close to the bottom: the gap between strategy (€5,765/mo median) and social media (€3,333/mo) is €2,432 per month in salary terms, which becomes a €225 spread in baseline day rates. The market pays for judgement it cannot easily replace.

Content & SEO is the sobering row. It is one of the largest specialisations in the survey (n=20) and starts at €375 per day, well under the overall median. High supply, heavy AI pressure, and a lot of freelancers competing on price. Social media sits at the floor, from €310 per day, which is exactly why so many social media freelancers burn out: many charge below even that.

The middle of the table is crowded. Growth, email, analytics, ops, and AI automation all start around €440 to €455 per day. If you sit there, differentiation comes from your track record and niche, not from the discipline's label.

05🚧

Why the calculated rate is your floor, not your price

The calculated rate is the minimum a freelance marketing consultant in Finland should charge, because it only matches an employee's salary. It does not pay you for anything an employer normally absorbs.

You carry the risk of a client cancelling with two weeks' notice. You eat the bench time between projects; 18 billable days per month assumes you actually have work every month. Your holiday pay exists only because the maths added it, and nobody tops it up if you take a fifth week. And none of it prices your expertise: a senior specialist solving in two days what takes an in-house team two weeks should not bill like a salary.

That is why the tables show a range rather than one number, and why the busiest specialists price at the top of their range or above it.

Working for foreign clients from Finland

Foreign clients change the maths, usually in your favour. A German, UK, or US company hiring a freelance marketer in Finland compares your quote to their local agency prices, not to Finnish salaries, so the Finnish break-even rate can look almost naive to them. Frequently asked questions

What is the average hourly rate for a freelance marketing consultant in Finland?

Between €70 and €115 per hour for ad-hoc hourly work at the median, starting from a €60 break-even against the median employed marketer's €4,658 monthly gross salary in 2026. Single hours carry the most overhead, so the hourly range tops out at double the baseline; full days run €520 to €650. By specialisation, hourly baselines go from €40 for social media to €70 for marketing strategy.

What day rate should a marketing freelancer charge in Finland?

Between €520 and €650 per day for the median profile, starting from a €435 break-even, based on the 2026 community median salary of €4,658 per month. Specialists vary: marketing strategy runs €645 to €805 per day (baseline €535), growth marketing €545 to €680, and content and SEO €450 to €565. Treat the starting figures as floors, not target prices.

What is YEL and why does it matter for freelance rates?

YEL is Finland's mandatory pension insurance for entrepreneurs, costing roughly 24% of confirmed income (about 19% with the new-entrepreneur discount during the first years). Employees get pension contributions paid by their employer, so freelancers must build YEL into their rates or quietly fall behind on pension.

Should junior freelancers charge less?

Somewhat, but less than most juniors think. Our data puts the junior baseline at €310 per day, with real quotes usually between €370 and €465, versus a €390 baseline for mid-level. Going far below the floor means earning less than an employed junior with none of the security. Compete on scope and speed, not on price.

Do these rates include VAT?

No. All rates on this page exclude VAT. Finnish freelancers add VAT on top of their invoices to Finnish business clients, who reclaim it. Quote your rates excluding VAT and say so clearly in every proposal.

How many billable days does a freelancer have per month?

About 18. A typical month has 21 working days; subtract roughly one public holiday, one sick day, and one admin day for invoicing, sales, and bookkeeping, and you land at 18. Freelancers who assume 21 billable days underprice by around 15%.

How much does a freelance SEO consultant charge in Finland?

A freelance SEO consultant in Finland charges €450 to €565 per day, starting from €375, or €60 to €100 per hour for ad-hoc work, mapped from the Content & SEO median salary in our 2026 survey. Senior technical SEO specialists with a proven track record often price closer to marketing strategy rates, €645 per day and up.

How much does a fractional CMO cost in Finland?

A fractional CMO in Finland bills €835 to €1,045 per day, starting from a €700 break-even, or €110 to €185 per hour for ad-hoc work, against an employed director's €7,500 monthly median salary. One to two days per week on a retainer is the usual format.
