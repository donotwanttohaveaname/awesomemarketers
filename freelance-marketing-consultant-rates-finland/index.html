Freelance marketing consultant hourly rates in Finland: what to charge in 2026

Real numbers, calculated from what 148 Finnish marketers actually earn.

By Anna Pogrebniak, Founder Published 23 July 2026 · Last updated 23 July 2026 7 min read

A freelance marketing consultant in Finland charges from €455 per day, typically up to €565, and from €115 per hour for ad-hoc hourly work, typically up to €135. Day rates sit just above break-even against the median employed marketer's €4,658 monthly gross salary in 2026; hourly work runs about double the day-rate equivalent. By specialisation, hourly rates start from €85 (social media) to €145 (marketing strategy).

Key takeaways

  • The median Finnish marketer: from €455/day (typically up to €565) and from €115/hour for ad-hoc work (typically up to €135).
  • Specialisation moves the starting rate from €325/day (social media) to €565/day (marketing strategy).
  • Seniority matters too: juniors start from €85/h, directors from €185/h for ad-hoc hourly work.
  • Day rates: "from" sits 5% above bare break-even and "typically up to" 30% above it. Hourly: "from" doubles break-even and "typically up to" adds 130%, because single hours are the most expensive way to buy a freelancer.
  • All figures exclude VAT and come from the Awesome Marketers Marketing Salary Survey Finland 2026 (148 respondents).

How much does a freelance marketing consultant charge in Finland?

A freelance marketing consultant in Finland charges from €455 per day, typically up to €565. Ad-hoc hourly work starts at €115 per hour, typically up to €135. The day figure comes from the 2026 median gross salary of €4,658 per month in the Awesome Marketers Marketing Salary Survey Finland 2026 (148 respondents), converted to a freelance rate with holiday pay and YEL pension included, plus a 5% minimum margin; hourly rates double the break-even, because single hours carry the most overhead. New freelancers on the discounted starter YEL rate can start slightly lower, from €425 per day or €110 per hour.

To be blunt: the "from" figures sit only 5% above break-even. They tell you what it costs to be your own employer at the median salary, plus the thinnest workable margin. Established freelancers in the community typically charge up to 30% above break-even, which is the "typically up to" figure in every table below, and top specialists stretch past 50%. If a client tells you €455 sounds expensive, they are comparing it to a salary. It is not a salary. It is a salary plus pension, holidays, and risk.

How we calculated these rates

We calculated these freelance rates the same way our freelance rate calculator does, starting from real salary data instead of a guess. The method, step by step:

  1. Start with the employee's monthly gross salary. Overall median in 2026: €4,658.
  2. Add holiday pay. Multiply by 14/11, because employees get roughly 14 months of pay over 11 working months (holiday pay plus the holiday bonus).
  3. Add YEL pension. Divide by 0.76 at the standard ~24% YEL rate. New freelancers get a starter discount of ~19%, so divide by 0.81 instead.
  4. Divide by 18 billable days per month. That is 21 working days minus roughly one public holiday, one sick day, and one admin day.
  5. Divide by 7.5 hours for the break-even hourly rate, then round to the nearest €5.

All figures exclude VAT. The result is your bare break-even: what you need to match an employed peer, nothing more. Day rates in the tables add a 5% minimum margin on top of that, with "typically up to" at +30%. Hourly rates add 100%, with "typically up to" at +130%: ad-hoc hours are the most expensive way to hire anyone, and booking full days is the discount. Marketing strategy tops the table from €145 per hour for ad-hoc work, typically up to €165, and it is not close to the bottom: the gap between strategy (€5,765/mo median) and social media (€3,333/mo) is €2,432 per month in salary terms, which becomes a €240 spread in starting day rates. The market pays for judgement it cannot easily replace.

Content & SEO is the sobering row. It is one of the largest specialisations in the survey (n=20) and starts at €395 per day, well under the overall median. High supply, heavy AI pressure, and a lot of freelancers competing on price. The middle of the table is crowded. Growth, email, analytics, ops, and AI automation all start around €465 to €475 per day. If you sit there, differentiation comes from your track record and niche, not from the discipline's label.

Why the calculated rate is your floor, not your price

The calculated rate is the minimum a freelance marketing consultant in Finland should charge, because it only matches an employee's salary. It does not pay you for anything an employer normally absorbs. You carry the risk of a client cancelling with two weeks' notice. You eat the bench time between projects; 18 billable days per month assumes you actually have work every month. Your holiday pay exists only because the maths added it, and nobody tops it up if you take a fifth week. And none of it prices your expertise: a senior specialist solving in two days what takes an in-house team two weeks should not bill like a salary.

That is why every "from" figure has a "typically up to" ceiling above it, and why top specialists charge 50% or more over break-even. Practical pricing rules from the community: retainers can sit closest to the floor because they buy you predictability; project fees should sit well above it because scope always creeps; and ad-hoc hourly is the most expensive way to buy you, which is why the hourly column runs about double the day rates. And remember every figure here excludes VAT, which you add on top of the invoice.

Working for foreign clients from Finland

Foreign clients change the maths, usually in your favour. A German, UK, or US company hiring a freelance marketer in Finland compares your quote to their local agency prices, not to Finnish salaries, so the Finnish break-even rate can look almost naive to them. Frequently asked questions

What is the average hourly rate for a freelance marketing consultant in Finland?

From €115 per hour for ad-hoc hourly work, typically up to €135. Hourly rates run about double the break-even against the median employed marketer's €4,658 monthly gross salary in 2026, because single hours carry the most overhead; full days start at €455. By specialisation, hourly rates start from €85 for social media and €145 for marketing strategy.

What day rate should a marketing freelancer charge in Finland?

From €455 per day at the standard YEL rate, typically up to €565, based on the 2026 community median salary of €4,658 per month. Specialists vary: marketing strategy starts at €565 per day (typically up to €695), growth marketing at €475, and content and SEO at €395. Treat the starting figures as floors, not target prices.

What is YEL and why does it matter for freelance rates?

YEL is Finland's mandatory pension insurance for entrepreneurs, costing roughly 24% of confirmed income (about 19% with the new-entrepreneur discount during the first years). Employees get pension contributions paid by their employer, so freelancers must build YEL into their rates or quietly fall behind on pension.

Should junior freelancers charge less?

Somewhat, but less than most juniors think. Our data puts the junior floor at €325 per day (€85 per hour for ad-hoc work), typically up to €405, versus €410 for mid-level. Going far below the floor means earning less than an employed junior with none of the security. Compete on scope and speed, not on price.

Do these rates include VAT?

No. All rates on this page exclude VAT. Finnish freelancers add VAT on top of their invoices to Finnish business clients, who reclaim it. Quote your rates excluding VAT and say so clearly in every proposal.

How many billable days does a freelancer have per month?

About 18. A typical month has 21 working days; subtract roughly one public holiday, one sick day, and one admin day for invoicing, sales, and bookkeeping, and you land at 18. Freelancers who assume 21 billable days underprice by around 15%.

How much does a freelance SEO consultant charge in Finland?

A freelance SEO consultant in Finland starts around €395 per day, or €100 per hour for ad-hoc work, typically up to €490 per day, mapped from the Content & SEO median salary in our 2026 survey. Senior technical SEO specialists with a proven track record often price closer to marketing strategy rates, €565 per day and up.

How much does a fractional CMO cost in Finland?

A fractional CMO in Finland starts at €735 per day, or €185 per hour for ad-hoc work, against an employed director's €7,500 monthly median salary, and typically bills up to €905 per day. One to two days per week on a retainer is the usual format.
