Frequently asked questions

How do I hire a marketing freelancer in Finland?

Message Anna Pogrebniak on LinkedIn or email anna@awesomemarketers.fi with what you need: the channel, the scope, your budget, and the working language. She asks The Awesome Marketers community (1,787+ marketers) and shortlists freelancers who actually fit. You then work with the freelancer directly.

How much does a marketing freelancer cost in Finland?

Typical day rates run about 30% above the €325 to €565 starting range, or €85 to €145 per hour for ad-hoc hourly work, excluding VAT, depending on specialisation and seniority. These figures come from our 2026 community salary survey of 148 marketers. Strategy consultants sit at the top of the range, social media freelancers at the bottom.

Does The Awesome Marketers charge for matching?

No. Matching is free for both sides. We take no commission and no cut of the freelancer's fee. We do it because it helps the community, and the community is the point.

What marketing specialisations are available?

Marketing strategy, growth, performance and paid advertising, content and SEO, brand and design, copywriting, PR and communications, analytics and marketing ops, email and CRM, social media, and AI and automation. Most freelancers in the community have years of in-house or agency experience in Finland.

Can you help with English-speaking freelancers?

Yes. The community is English-first, so nearly every freelancer works in English. Many also work in Finnish, Swedish, or other languages. Mention language requirements in your brief and we match accordingly.
